BarcodeMate 0.5.0 adds **Ideas & bug reports**, a feedback area shared with barcodemate.com.

- Submit text and optional images. Choose private (the default) or public; public feedback appears immediately on the feedback board. Contact email is always private.
- Upload original PNG, JPEG or WebP images, up to 50 MiB each and 80 MiB per submission, without a fixed image-count limit. The server stores compressed display images and separate thumbnails, then discards the originals.
- View up to five thumbnails in a row; larger collections use a carousel. Open a thumbnail to view a larger image.
- Withdraw your feedback and all its images from the device used to submit it. Clearing app/browser data removes withdrawal access.
- Available in all 24 interface languages, with light and dark themes. Feedback is sent only when you choose Submit; projects and barcode data are not uploaded automatically.

Source commit: `SOURCE_COMMIT`. Validated Windows/macOS packages: https://github.com/barcodemate/desktop/actions/runs/SOURCE_RUN

Installers remain unsigned and macOS packages are not notarized.
